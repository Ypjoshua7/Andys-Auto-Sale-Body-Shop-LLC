Our Story

Founded in 2003, AutoPremier started as a small family-owned dealership with a simple mission: provide quality vehicles and honest service to our community.

Over two decades later, we've grown into a full-service automotive center, but our values remain the same. We believe everyone deserves a reliable vehicle and fair treatment, regardless of their credit history.

Today, we're proud to offer a wide selection of quality pre-owned vehicles, expert body shop services, and flexible in-house financing options that help customers from all walks of life get on the road.
